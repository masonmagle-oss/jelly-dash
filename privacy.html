Squishy Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Squishy Dash is published by Light Takes Root LLC. This policy explains what the game does with information.

What we collect

Nothing. Squishy Dash has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, receive or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and any levels you build are saved in your device's own storage. They never leave your device unless you copy a level code or save code and share it yourself.

Third parties

The game makes no requests to third-party services. Fonts, sound effects and music are included in the app.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game removes everything it saved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: studio@lighttakesroot.com
